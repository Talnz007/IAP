# Product

## Register
product

## Users
Residents of Islamabad, environmental researchers, and health-conscious individuals who need reliable and beautiful air quality data.

## Product Purpose
Display current and predicted AQI metrics through a reliable, beautifully designed dashboard that users want to keep open on their screens.

## Brand Personality
Dark Academia, intellectual, elegant, atmospheric, and scholarly.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS dashboards
- Vibrant neon themes or default web colors (plain red/blue/green)
- Simple minimal viable products without aesthetic depth

## Design Principles
- **Sophisticated Restraint**: Use deep, atmospheric colors rather than stark contrasts to create a calm, focused environment.
- **Living Interface**: Elements should feel responsive and alive through subtle micro-animations that don't overwhelm.
- **Clarity through Typography**: Use hierarchy and scale to present complex data cleanly, evoking an editorial or scholarly feel.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Maintain sufficient contrast for readability in dark mode environments. Avoid frantic animations or purely decorative elements that distract from the core data.
